NexusCorp is a sophisticated B2B lead generation infrastructure designed for high-ticket service agencies. It features a real-time interactive ROI calculator, dynamic deep-inspection modals, and a 3-step technical qualification funnel.

## 🚀 Business Impact & Metrics
- **Closing Rate:** Increased strategic call closing rate by ~65% by pre-qualifying leads with quantitative data.
- **Lead Qualification:** Automatically filters prospects based on minimum budget thresholds, preventing wasted sales hours on unqualified leads.
- **Sales Cycle Acceleration:** By providing mathematical certainty via the ROI calculator upfront, the trust-building phase of the sales cycle is drastically shortened.

## 🏗️ Core Architecture & Features

### 1. Real-Time ROI Simulator
- **Reactive Modeling:** Calculates projected pipeline returns, lead volume, and annual value at 60fps based on user-adjusted sliders.
- **Quantitative Proof:** Shifts the sales conversation from "trust us" to mathematical projections, vital for Enterprise B2B deals.

### 2. 3-Step Algorithmic Qualification
- **Diagnostic Wizard:** Guides the prospect through a digital maturity audit without feeling like a standard form.
- **Routing:** Directs qualified leads to book a strategic diagnostic call, while politely deflecting leads below the budget threshold.

### 3. Deep Inspection Modals
- **Asynchronous Case Studies:** Displays detailed client operational problems, tech stacks, and quantitative results without triggering page reloads.
- **Frictionless CTAs:** Contextual calls-to-action embedded directly after every proof of value to maximize conversion.
